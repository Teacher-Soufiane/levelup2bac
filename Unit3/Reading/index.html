UNIT3: READING EDUCATION QUIZ-1
1Literacy lights the way through three paths: formal, non-formal, and informal education. Formal education is planned and monitored by the authorities, and students receive official certificates or diplomas recognized by employers. Non-formal education, like adult literacy classes, is organized but takes place outside the traditional school system. These programs are practical, like teaching a farmer to read farm instructions.
2Informal education is the least structured type, yet it may be the most constant throughout a person's life. It happens through daily experiences: conversations, observing others, or using media. These experiences shape vocabulary and communication skills from early childhood. The three types do not compete; they complement each other. Together, they keep literacy accessible to everyone.
